# Narragansett Bay source inventory

Audit date 2026-11-21 UTC. This inventory distinguishes published methods and metadata, actual files or rows read during the audit, and records outside the reviewed channels. Geographic coordinates are expressed as latitude and longitude. The inspected records do not establish a common named horizontal datum for the two plotted sites.

## RIDEM Narragansett Bay Fixed-Site Monitoring Network

- **Provider and identity.** Rhode Island Department of Environmental Management, Office of Water Resources, with NBC as the Bullock Reach operator. Reviewed 2012 edited station B4-S, agency code `nbcbs`, sheet `BR` of `B4.nbcBR2012.editted.xls`. Workbook Notes lists 41°44.434′ N, 71°22.480′ W, approximately 41.74057, −71.37467. The NBC annual report gives a different approximate coordinate. The datum and reason for the difference remain unresolved. [Network archives and citations](https://dem.ri.gov/environmental-protection-bureau/water-resources/research-monitoring/narraganset-bay-assessment-data)
- **Access and actual coverage.** Downloaded the 129 MiB annual archive at 00:28:32 UTC. Inspected the nested edited workbook and surface metadata. The 194 workbook days span 2012-05-17 through 2012-11-26 inclusive, with 18,624 unique nominal quarter-hour rows and 18,517 finite surface temperature cells. The source ZIP also contains raw and corrected products. The raw and corrected B4 values were outside this audit. [2012 archive](https://datadem.ri.gov/documents/bart/nbfsmn12.zip)
- **Method and QA.** Fixed near-surface sonde approximately 0.5–1 m below the surface, with separate mid and bottom sondes. The workbook records temperature (°C), depth (m), conductivity (mS/cm), salinity (ppt), dissolved oxygen (% and mg/L), pH and optical variables. The B4-S reviewed deployment is seasonal. Generic network May–October guidance and other stations’ winter programs do not replace this station’s dates. RIDEM distinguishes raw instrument files, edited data with deletions, and corrected products that can contain filled or adjusted values. The audited temperature column has no bold numeric cells marked as deleted and calculated under the workbook’s notes. This is a release-coverage check with no independent sensor QA.
- **Attribution and limits.** Cite Narragansett Bay Fixed-Site Monitoring Network (NBFSMN), 2012, 2012 Datasets, RIDEM Office of Water Resources. The app includes a derived coverage projection and worksheet references. No raw workbook or input temperature values are redistributed by Strata. Workbook dates and times are preserved as local labels at their published precision. No UTC offset was verified for that worksheet.

## Narragansett Bay Commission environmental monitoring

- **Provider and identity.** NBC Environmental Monitoring and Data Analysis. The 2012 report covers B4 Bullock Reach and Phillipsdale Landing, alongside separate weekly Providence and Seekonk River mapping. B4 report coordinate is approximately 41°43.944′ N, 71°22.214′ W, or 41.73240, −71.37023. The report section does not specify a named datum. [2012 NBC report, section 5, pp. 35–37](https://www.narrabay.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/2012_EMDA_Data_Report.pdf)
- **Access and scope.** The annual PDF and Snapshot portal were reachable. The report confirms the B4 deployment dates and nominal 15-minute schedule. Its B4 near-surface, mid-depth, and bottom methods and calibration notes help interpret the RIDEM archive. The report and workbook are evidence from the same buoy program. NBC's separate weekly surface mapping tracks temperature, salinity, DO and chlorophyll. The audit located its description and reports. A historical row-level export, row dates, dataset IDs, and a reuse grant for that separate series were not verified in the reviewed channels. [Program and report index](https://www.narrabay.com/programs-and-initiatives/environmental-monitoring/) · [Snapshot portal](https://www.narrabay.com/snapshot-of-the-bay/)
- **Attribution and limits.** Attribute NBC reports and supplied documentation. This slice paraphrases reported methods and uses the network release to count B4 records. It does not count a missing NBC export as zero observations.

## URI Graduate School of Oceanography Fish Trawl Survey

- **Provider and identity.** URI-GSO and RIDEM Division of Marine Fisheries. Fox Island (41°34.5′ N, 71°24.3′ W) and Whale Rock (41°26.3′ N, 71°25.4′ W). Methods provide geographic coordinates and bottom depth ranges of 6.1–7.9 m and 19.8–25.9 m respectively at low tide. The datum is not stated on the methods page. Visits are nominally one morning per week, year-round. Methods describe depth-specific surface/bottom temperature, salinity, conductivity and dissolved oxygen sampling, with sonde use changing during the long record. [URI methods](https://web.uri.edu/gso/research/fish-trawl/methods/)
- **Access and actual coverage.** Both environmental download labels on the page pointed to the same `Weekly-Temperature-Data_1959-2024.xlsx` URL. An actual copy retrieved at 00:27:47 UTC contained Fox Island and Whale Rock sheets with Date, Station, Surface Temperature and Bottom Temperature. Date rows on both sheets span 1959-01-21 through 2024-12-31. Date rows may have blank values. No separate conductivity, salinity or DO workbook was verified through those links. [URI downloads](https://web.uri.edu/gso/research/fish-trawl/data/)
- **QA and rights.** The policy describes estimated or substituted values during 2017 and 2020 interruptions. Some Fox Island values were filled from the URI plankton survey, so independence is period-specific. It asks users to cite URI-GSO and RIDEM and restricts third-party data distribution. The investigation includes links and schema-level audit facts. No workbook rows are redistributed or pooled. [URI data use policy](https://web.uri.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/916/fish-trawl-data-use-policy-letter.pdf)

## URI Narragansett Bay Long-Term Plankton Time Series

- **Provider and identity.** URI-GSO, BCO-DMO dataset **874956 v1**, DOI **10.26008/1912/bco-dmo.874956.1**. Station II, West Passage, centroid 41.56861° N, 71.39194° W. The release gives geographic degrees, with no named datum in the reviewed metadata. Surface bucket temperature (°C) and salinity are nominally weekly. The release also contains irradiance, nutrients, precipitation, Secchi depth and chlorophyll. It supplies `year,week` keys without calendar dates, time of day or timezone. The release timing does not establish identical schedules for every variable. [Dataset and parameter descriptions](https://www.bco-dmo.org/dataset/874956)
- **Access and actual coverage.** Downloaded the 174,503-byte original CSV at 00:26:51 UTC. The reviewed temperature field is `Temperature_SURF`, with `nd` indicating absence in the file. Nonmissing entries span 1959 week label 1 to 2019 week label 52. The in-scope 2011, 2012 and 2013 counts are 32, 0 and 46 nonmissing entries out of 52 distinct archive weeks each. Metadata explicitly lists a 2012 temperature collection gap. A secondary audit found 16 nonmissing values in 1997 and 42 in 1999 despite the methods describing a 1997–1999 temperature collection gap. Their provenance or reconstruction status needs clarification. These years are outside the 2012 investigation. [Original CSV](https://datadocs.bco-dmo.org/dataset/874956/file/5AAMkwXSglPxE4/nbpts_data_1959-2019_final.csv)
- **QA and rights.** BCO-DMO marks version 1 validated and final, with missing-value and methods notes. Attribution goes to Tatiana Rynearson and Patricia Thibodeau, URI-GSO, with the dataset title, DOI, version and BCO-DMO repository. The app counts presence and removes measurement values. The URI project requests citation by full series name and the access date. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [URI series attribution](https://web.uri.edu/gso/research/plankton/data/)

## Brown Narragansett Bay Data Explorer

- **Provider and identity.** Rhode Island Data Discovery Center (RIDDC), RI Buoy Data ERDDAP dataset `combined_e784_bee5_492e`, compiling the RIDEM-led network. Metadata uses CF/ACDD geographic degrees and UTC timestamps, but a named datum was not verified. The dataset-wide time extent is 2003-05-22 09:30 UTC to 2023-12-31 23:45 UTC. This is an extent across stations and variables. [Brown credits](https://riddc.brown.edu/) · [ERDDAP schema and license](https://erddap.riddc.brown.edu/erddap/tabledap/combined_e784_bee5_492e.html)
- **Access and actual coverage.** Queried the station-coordinate table and one day of surface temperature for `bid5`, at 41.731° N, 71.3653° W. The 96 retrieved rows for 2012-07-01 00:00–23:45 UTC validate the time and °C fields for that scope. They do not establish a crosswalk to B4-S. The separately documented Quonset Pt dataset `raw_59d1_0a18_37ba` exposes `SalinitySurface`. It does not serve the surface-temperature field through that dataset. [Test query](https://erddap.riddc.brown.edu/erddap/tabledap/combined_e784_bee5_492e.csv?time,station_name,latitude,longitude,WaterTempSurface&station_name=%22bid5%22&time%3E=2012-07-01T00%3A00%3A00Z&time%3C2012-07-02T00%3A00%3A00Z) · [Quonset Pt metadata](https://erddap.riddc.brown.edu/erddap/tabledap/raw_59d1_0a18_37ba.html)
- **QA and rights.** Brown credits the network's quality-controlled historical inputs. The combined payload exposes published values without a verified per-cell quality or correction crosswalk for the selected B4 workbook. The ERDDAP license permits free redistribution with accuracy and legal-use limitations, and the project requests citations to the original agency sources. No Brown rows are used to fill the comparison.

## Compatibility decision

The weekly Station II bucket samples and B4 near-surface quarter-hour sonde values share Celsius units and overlap in 2012 but differ in site, depth, method and time support. No matched observations or paired calibration were established. NBC reports and Brown's buoy explorer also overlap the RIDEM network by provenance. The instrument compares source coverage and uncertainty only.
