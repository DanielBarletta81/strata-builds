# STRATA-014 Phase 3 · Ancient Seaway Laboratory

The 111 Ma Tethys chapter lets visitors compare the frozen global reconstruction with four regional evidence records. Plate Reconstruction shows the Phase 2 map. Regional Marine Evidence inspects published outcrops, a pelagic section and core, and a regional kinematic restoration. Open Questions identifies what would resolve the remaining gaps. The records constrain depositional environments at particular intervals and sites. The extent of exposed land, the depth of a basin, and the path of a 111 Ma seaway remain unresolved.

### The 111 Ma time comparison

Ramos et al. report **111.70 ± 0.12 Ma** for Urbino and **111.28 ± 0.12 Ma** for Leenhardt in their conclusions. The graphic shows these estimates and the quoted uncertainty separately from the fixed 111 Ma plate-model time. The ages are based on astronomical tuning and correlated age anchors. No local radiometric dates were available to the study for this core interval. Table 3 reports different Leenhardt estimates of **111.40, 111.42 and 111.44 Ma**. The lab preserves the discrepancy and does not infer its cause. Earlier age models also give different estimates. Neither these horizons nor the whole study interval quantify water-column oxygen at 111 Ma. [Ramos et al., §§3.3–3.4 and 4.3, Table 3 and conclusions](https://doi.org/10.1029/2024PA004860)

The linked Zenodo deposit documents magnetic susceptibility (`PLG MS.txt`, MD5 `2954bd3adc6144b65c187bbe370362a6`) and normalized Fe and Ti (`PLG_Fe_norm.txt`, `4d56afef679e9e07172d4ec4367ce365`, and `PLG_Ti_norm.txt`, `2dc63bb066365e7fa3981d79587b2ed7`). These are publisher-supplied file checksums from the repository listing and API. The prototype does not ingest those three files or derive new ages from them. [Versioned deposit](https://zenodo.org/records/10557295) · [License and file API](https://zenodo.org/api/records/10557295)

### Regional geographic validation

The benchmark uses one pre-existing representative modern point, 16.5° E, 41.1° N, from the Phase 2 Apulia checkpoint. No field locality is assigned this location. At 111 Ma, two regional features containing the modern point share plate 3089: Apulia and Apulian platform and Pre-Apulian slope. Phase 2 uses plate 3307. The regional circuit at 111 Ma follows 3089 → 715 → 701. Both models independently use their Southern Africa anchor, 701. The regional point rotation and feature-reconstruction API agree within 0.000001° in the same pyGPlates numerical core.

A placement needs a source-defined point or boundary, local structural restoration, dated continuity at the target horizon, source rights, and a validated transform into a named circuit and reference. The current records lack the full set of evidence needed for a geographic overlay. Named localities therefore stay in the evidence table. Age coverage and spatial confidence are assessed separately. The overlay remains unavailable even where the marine depositional record is well supported. The absence of coverage leaves conditions open.

## Ancient oceanography and Phase 4 candidates

A shallow sill can restrict exchange, density stratification can slow vertical oxygen renewal, and respiration can increase the demand for oxygen. Flow and bottom redox can modify sediment delivery and preservation. Light, sediment supply and water chemistry affect carbonate production. Currents and passage geometry can influence the dispersal of organisms with planktonic stages. These are possible mechanisms. The two geological archives do not validate 111 Ma boundary conditions for an ocean simulation. Sedimentary facies, bathymetry, circulation, geochemistry and ecosystems each need their own evidence. [Modern sill and renewal observations](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmars.2022.1001146) · [Sediment process synthesis](https://doi.org/10.5194/bg-6-1273-2009)

1. Start with one correlated, bed-level transect linking the Apulian platform to its margin. Acquire source logs, sample or horizon IDs, calibrated age ties, section coordinates and datum, deformation corrections and geometry rights. Restore at least several corresponding points in both circuits and quantify residuals before publishing candidate geographic constraints.
2. Inventory rudist and oyster or other bivalve assemblages by museum record or figured specimen, bed, age, taxonomic authority and taphonomy. The known Gargano oyster-like *Chondrodonta* study treats the **lower Aptian** and OAE 1a. It can inform a research question and supplies no occurrence at 111 Ma. Test proposed carbonate food-web links against tiering, traces, feeding evidence and coeval assemblages. Require the same age and locality checks for marine reptiles and other fauna. No species are plotted in Phase 3. [Primary Gargano bivalve study](https://doi.org/10.1111/sed.12887) · [Rudist-bearing Calcare di Bari sections](https://doi.org/10.3301/IJG.2010.18)
3. Treat OAE 1b as multiple reported horizons whose ages and correlations need rechecking. Compare age-tied records of sulfur phases and isotopes, iron speciation, organic carbon, sedimentology and preservation at several sites before inferring anoxia or sulfur cycling. Test local bottom-water restriction against regional changes in productivity or ventilation. [Poggio le Guaine section and correlations](https://www.micropress.org/microaccess/stratigraphy/issue-312/article-1907)
4. Keep modern vent analogues separate from fossil evidence for chemosynthetic ecosystems. Require age-appropriate deposits, mineral and isotope evidence and diagnostic fossils with taphonomic context. Published Italian Miocene methane-seep bivalves illustrate a fossil study design and are far younger. Gaps in preservation do not establish ecological absence. World of Tethys material is outside the primary evidence register. [Original fossil seep study](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-paleontology/article/chemosymbiotic-bivalves-from-miocene-methaneseep-carbonates-in-italy/F731B3DE7AF42162D4EF1FA2AE13747F)

## Architecture and review

The [Tethys chapter](../src/features/deep-time/tethys/chapter.tsx) maintains its existing 111 Ma gate. The laboratory is loaded only for that selection. Its reducer preserves the selected record across Marine Evidence and Open Questions and links supported contextual checkpoints to the evidence register. Poggio le Guaine leaves the last map checkpoint in place and discloses that the section has no validated map locator. The original map retains all 193 lithosphere parts, 85 boundary segments and eleven checkpoints. The prototype has no new runtime dependencies or upstream fetches.

The source review cross-checks facts with publications and institutional material. The spatial check uses one independent regional reconstruction and the same numerical library. An external expert review of the chosen field correlations remains pending.
