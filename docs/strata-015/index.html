01 / Five directions.
One recommendation.

Leading candidate: refined Concept E uses equal-area slabs and a wider S-shaped fracture. D retains the rocket-bolt alternative. All remain proposals for review.

Concept E / Refined leading candidate

Fractured strata

Two interlocking slabs. One continuous S-shaped fracture. The letter comes from the gap, with clipped stone corners and no fine texture to disappear at small sizes.

16px24px32px48px

Recommended default: refined E with the unframed wordmark. The header previews this candidate; final identity approval is pending.

Concept D / Owner-requested exploration

Rocket-bolt S

A single angular path folds like an S, with an ascending nose and a cut tail. No tiny window, flame or detached ornament to disappear at favicon size.

16px24px32px48px

New direction for review. More energetic than the strata mark; its arrow/lightning reading is stronger than its literal S at 16 px.

Concept A / Earlier direction

Offset strata

Three cut layers share a measured offset. A stepped negative-space seam carries the identity from symbol to section dividers. No literal landscape.

16px24px32px48px

Most distinctive silhouette in this set; the middle gap must be checked at 16 px.

Concept B / Alternative

Reference section

A cropped registration frame surrounds two layers. A useful inspection metaphor for a scientific instrument.

16px24px32px48px

Precise, but the outer corners compete with the layers at small sizes and can read as a scanner.

Concept C / Alternative

Layered signal

Three staggered bands with measured breaks suggest independent layers of information without depicting scientific measurements.

16px24px32px48px

Simple and reproducible, but closest to common stack/database symbols.

02 / Sharper lettering, clearer scale

Single-color reproduction and an outlined horizontal wordmark.

Revised horizontal lockup. Larger Source Sans 3 Semibold outlines with open counters; no runtime font dependency.

Use a single color. Preserve the internal seams; no glow or gradient needed.

Room to read

Clear space: ¼ symbol width on every side. Minimum symbol: 16 px. Revised lockup: 192 px minimum; 248 px in the header.

Favicon/app-icon integration remains gated. This review page alone uses the concept favicon.

Lettering revision

The earlier block letters had tight counters. The new lettering keeps the R and A openings clear and gives the name more room.

04 / Readable by design

Source Sans 3 / Source Code Pro. Locally served, licensed review specimens.

Display · 40–96 px

The record comes first.

A readable humanist sans for headings and explanation; a monospaced companion for units and coordinates. Captions stay useful, not microscopic.

Modeled age · existing application value

111 Ma

Published age bracket: 112.80–110.70 Ma. An age model is not an observation at every instant.

05 / Evidence has a name

Observed in the source

Solid rule and explicit label. Describes a reported record, never an inferred measurement.

Scientific interpretation

Double rule and explicit label. Keep inference separate from the observation.

Still unresolved

Dashed rule and explicit label. Missing evidence remains visible.

Synthetic demonstration

Dotted rule and persistent text. Synthetic data must never inherit “observed” styling.
