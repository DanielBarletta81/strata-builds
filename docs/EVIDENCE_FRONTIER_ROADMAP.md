# Evidence Frontier roadmap

STRATA-004 implements an hourly Gap Inspector for NOAA CO-OPS station 8454000 and a typed contract for later work. The current instrument compares accepted timestamps with a review grid, exposes rejected raw rows, preserves unresolved quality fields, and marks ambiguous responses as unknown coverage. Its findings apply to the retrieved response and selected window.

## Future investigations

| Capability | Evidence to record | Next investigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Geographic blind spots | Stated monitoring goals, locations and dates of documented sampling, survey effort, spatial resolution, and terrain or access limits | Check regional inventories and ask whether other providers sampled the same location using a comparable method. |
| Temporal gaps | Expected cadence, observation intervals, known changes in monitoring, seasonal coverage, and unavailable periods | Compare metadata, maintenance history, and independent time series to distinguish a publication gap from a documented break. |
| Aging research | Original methods and conditions, subsequent changes, and any replication or follow-up | Specify which change warrants remeasurement and what result could challenge the earlier finding. Study age alone is insufficient grounds. |
| Methodological incompatibility | Instruments, detection limits, scales, definitions, sampling design, and normalization assumptions | Identify a cross-calibration, shared reference, or paired measurement that could make the studies comparable. |
| Contradictory findings | The exact claims, underlying observations, uncertainty, and differences in population, period, methods, or conditions | Design follow-up evidence that can distinguish methodological variation, sampling variation, and real change. |
| Field knowledge | Documented observations from fishermen, field researchers, and local communities, including date, location, attribution or consent, and whether the account is firsthand | Develop a testable question and seek independent observations or targeted sampling. |
| Data discoverability | Dataset identifiers, catalog and archive search history, available metadata, access restrictions, provenance, and reuse permissions | Contact the custodian, locate accessible archived records, or establish whether the original data can be reused. |

## What qualifies as a research gap

Define the research question and scope, record the catalogs, providers, and literature checked, and document why the sources do or do not address the question. Record the relevant dates, methods, inclusion criteria, unavailable records, and remaining alternatives. A confirmed research gap depends on this cross-source review and its stated limits. Where a search is incomplete, record incomplete discovery and identify which source could change the assessment. Retain source links, retrieval dates, and verification outcomes.

The contract requires multiple traceable sources and a review statement for a research gap. STRATA-004 emits only source-scoped findings from NOAA. Literature scanning, automated causal inference, and research rankings remain future scope.
