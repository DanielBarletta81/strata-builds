## Study periods

- **Unstocked, 1995–1998.** Baseline samples previously collected by Alan Libby. Table 1 covers spring electrofishing.
- **Stocked, 1999–2002.** Samples across five selected freshwater systems. Table 1 covers fall angling and fall electrofishing in separate series.
- **1999 management question.** The initiative asked whether supplemental stocking increased the number of legal-size bass per acre. Answering it requires the historical legal-size definition and comparable abundance estimates per acre, with effort and sampling context. The available tables support comparison of the printed estimates within their stated scope.

## Interpretation boundary

The photographed Discussion (`IMG_3948.jpg`–`IMG_3950.jpg`) identifies competition, forage limitations and gear selectivity as possible explanations. Those hypotheses require additional evidence at pond level. The 2026 questions in the UI are proposed checks, such as whether matched seasonal and gear coverage yields comparable estimates and whether prey abundance or fish density can distinguish ecological explanations.
