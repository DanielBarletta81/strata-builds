The chart uses columns H and K from the same row, preserving nitrate + nitrite in ppb-N. The source also reports separate nitrate, nitrite, NH₃ (ppb-N), orthophosphate (ppb-P), silicate (ppb-Si), total nitrogen and dissolved nitrogen. The 2022 methods report describes near-surface Niskin grabs, field salinity at collection, 0.45 µm filtration for dissolved nutrients and EPA Method 353.4 for the combined analyte. The 2025 workbook does not provide a complete analyte-by-date method detection limit series. The selected combined field includes reported limits of `<6.000` ppb-N. Those tokens remain visible and are never substituted numerically. Original clock UTC offset is unverified. A colored legend distinguishes calculated cells, blank reviews, NM (not measured), and NR (not reportable). Exact routine surface labels exclude field blanks, duplicate samples and bottom grabs. The associated blanks are used to classify the Phillipsdale sampling location only. Records carrying review comments remain inspectable and outside the scatterplot. ## Depth profiles and coverage

A single common archive window, **July 10, 2012, 08:00–12:00 inclusive**, contributes 17 slots per sensor. The network’s processed files normalize `Date & Time` to local standard time and quarter hours. Each layer is keyed independently by its date and clock cells. Those labels support a comparison of published slots while leaving second-level synchronization unverified. The published units are ppt, mg/L dissolved oxygen, °C, and m of sensor depth. YSI sondes sample discrete layers, with two depths at Phillipsdale, Conimicut and T-Wharf, three at Bullock Reach and one at the GSO dock. Time gaps remain per variable. An edited workbook can still flag suspect or calculated values. The extraction interprets bold and the suspect cell style using each workbook’s Notes, and coverage counts unmarked numeric values. The slice has no missing layer-time rows or duplicate layer-time keys. Blanks are present within rows.

## Rights and publication boundaries

RIDEM prescribes citation by archive year and station. For this slice cite Narragansett Bay Fixed-Site Monitoring Network, 2012 Datasets, Rhode Island Department of Environmental Management, Office of Water Resources. NBC provides its sheets publicly, and URI names both MERL workbooks as share downloads and provides citation guidance. No explicit broad redistribution license was verified for the original workbooks. The repository retains bounded factual observation fields, locators and derived coverage with attribution. Extensive provider notes, full workbooks and archive files stay with the providers. CHRP is represented by its audit findings rather than individual rows. Confirm broader redistribution terms and methods before expanding the extracts.
